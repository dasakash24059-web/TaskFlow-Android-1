How TaskFlow Works

Sign In Securely

Launch TaskFlow on your Android phone and sign in with one tap using your Google Account via official Android Credential Manager.

Choose an Available Task

Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

TaskFlow collects minimal account credentials via Google Sign-In (display name, email address, and UID) to authenticate your user profile and track completed tasks.

2. Proof Submissions

When you submit proof for a task, your written response and uploaded screenshot image are securely stored to allow authorized administrators to verify task completion. We do not sell your personal data or submitted screenshots to third parties.

3. Security

User balances and submission records are protected with Firestore zero-trust security rules and role-based access controls to prevent unauthorized access or modification.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding your data or account deletion, please reach out via the in-app support chat or contact our support team.
